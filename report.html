Report a wrong card

The extension showed the wrong card, or said it couldn't identify one that's clearly visible? A quick report helps improve recognition.

Please include

  1. Video link with timestamp (on YouTube: right-click the video, "Copy video URL at current time").
  2. Which card you hovered: its position (e.g. "opponent's leader, top left") or its name/number if you know it.
  3. What the extension showed: the wrong card's name, or the message ("Couldn't identify this card", "Too low quality", "Card is obstructed", "DON!! card").
  4. Video quality you were watching at (720p, 1080p...) and whether the video was paused.
  5. Optional: a screenshot. Please don't include personal information.

Send it

Open a GitHub issue

A GitHub issue is public and needs a free GitHub account.

Before reporting

  • Set the video to 1080p. Small cards at 720p or lower are often unreadable.
  • Aim at the middle of the card and hold still for a moment.
  • Getting wrong matches often? Raise "Strictness" in the extension's popup.

Reports are only used to improve card recognition. See the privacy policy.
