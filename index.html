CardPeek

Hover a One Piece Card Game card in a YouTube or Twitch video and a sharp, readable copy of the card pops up next to your cursor, in English or Japanese.

How it works

  • Rest your cursor on the middle of a card in the video (live or paused).
  • The card is recognised on your own computer. Video frames never leave your browser.
  • The pop-up shows the card's name, stats, effect and a "Buy on TCGplayer" button with the market price.
  • If a card can't be identified, a short note says why: a DON!! card, obstructed (hand, marker or glare), too low quality, or not identified.
  • Alt+O turns it on or off. Esc hides the card.

Tips

  • Set the video to 1080p for overhead table cameras.
  • Hover both players' leaders first: other cards are then matched only within those decks' colours, which avoids most lookalike mistakes.
  • Raise "Strictness" in the extension's popup if you see wrong matches.
